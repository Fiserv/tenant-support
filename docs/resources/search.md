# Search

Developer Studio will do a full text search on tenant's data in the back end. DevStudio users can query and filter the data in many forms, such as by Capability, Region, Business type, etc.

This is using our new Mongo Atlas integration with improved aggregations and fuzzy search to allow users to find answers they may want even with misspellings or other intents. As of the moment, we do not integrate AI into our Search functionality but the AI Assistant built into our portal should have enough information and context to perform functionality similar to search.

To use AI Assistant, please click `Let me assist you!` at the top banner to open the chat window and login with a free Developer Studio account.
